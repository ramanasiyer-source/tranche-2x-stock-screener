About Tranche 2x

The research, the author, and the machine learning model behind the predictions.

What is this site about?

Tranche 2x is an experimental platform designed to identify mispricings in the small to mid-cap equity markets. Our goal is to surface companies exhibiting historical patterns associated with significant capital appreciation over a 12-month horizon.

About the Author

Built by a Quantitative Researcher exploring the intersection of modern machine learning and classical fundamental analysis. This project serves as a proving ground for robust feature engineering and predictive modeling in noisy financial environments.

About the Model

The core engine is a sophisticated machine learning pipeline combining gradient boosted decision trees (LightGBM and XGBoost) in a soft-voting ensemble.

The model processes over 20 distinct features for every stock in the universe. This includes complex valuation multiples, relative price momentum indicators, macroeconomic sensitivity, and sector-relative normalization parameters.

Rather than a black box, the model utilizes SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) to calculate exactly which fundamental or technical factors contributed to a stock's overall propensity score, bringing complete transparency to the predictions.
